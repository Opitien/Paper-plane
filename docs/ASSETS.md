# Paper Plane — Assets and Graphical Resources

Status: Milestone 0. Everything ships procedural first. Each row has a swap
point: the single file to change if a real asset replaces the generated one.

Guiding rule from the brief: do not buy or commission assets until the gameplay
is proven fun. Upgrade art after the first playable prototype.

## 2. Procedural paper plane: build notes

The dart is built from six planar faces so the silhouette reads as folded paper
from behind and from the side:

```
            nose
             /\
            /  \         top view: two wing panels meeting at a central crease
           /    \
          / | |  \       side view: a keel hangs below the crease, deepest at the tail
         /  | |   \
        /___|_|____\
      wing  keel  wing
```

- Faces: left wing, right wing, left keel, right keel, two tiny nose panels so
  the tip is not a single sharp vertex.
- Wings have a small dihedral (tips lifted about 8 degrees) so light catches
  them differently and the bank is readable.
- Crease lines are drawn as faint darker strokes in the generated roughness
  map, not as separate geometry.
- Edge imperfection: jitter the trailing edge vertices by up to 1 percent of
  length so the plane does not look machine-cut.
- Normals are computed per face (flat shading) to keep the folded-paper look.
- Pivot at roughly 40 percent of the length from the nose so pitching looks
  natural.
- Subtle animation (M4): wing tips flex with pitch rate and turbulence via a
  small vertex displacement in a custom shader chunk, or by rotating the two
  wing faces about the crease by a few degrees. The second option is simpler
  and preferred.

## 3. Sky palette table

Five keyframes indexed by progress. Each has zenith, horizon, ground, sun
colour, sun elevation, fog density and cloud tint. Interpolated linearly
between neighbours. Starting values to tune in M3:

| Stage | Progress | Zenith | Horizon | Sun elevation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Clear | 0.00 | deep sky blue | pale blue-white | high |
| Cumulus | 0.25 | sky blue | warm white | medium-high |
| Golden hour | 0.50 | dusty violet | orange-pink | low |
| Overcast | 0.72 | slate grey-blue | pale grey | hidden |
| Storm | 1.00 | charcoal blue | dark grey | hidden |

## 4. Where to get real assets later (if wanted)

- Sketchfab: paper airplane models. Check licence per model.
- Poly Haven: free HDRIs and textures (CC0). Only useful if the progression
  design changes.
- ambientCG: free paper textures (CC0).
- Kenney: UI sounds and assets (CC0).
- Freesound: wind loops. Check licence per sound.

Any real asset added must be listed here with its licence and source URL.
